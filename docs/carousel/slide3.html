System Architecture The 4-Stage Production Pipeline
Slide 3 of 6
STAGE 01 src/ingest.py

Ingestion & Normalization

  • ▹ 1-Indexed Offsets: Maps PyPDF page_idx to physical page + 1.
  • ▹ SHA-256 Digest: Prevents redundant document re-indexing.
  • ▹ Scanned PDF Guard: Density checks reject non-extractable PDFs.
STAGE 02 FastEmbed ONNX

Quantized Embeddings

  • ▹ Model: BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5 (384-dimensional).
  • ▹ Pure ONNX Runtime: Strips 800MB PyTorch dependency.
  • ▹ Low Latency: Cold-start in 2.22s, peaks at 136MB RAM delta.
STAGE 03 src/retriever.py

Ephemeral Chroma Store

  • ▹ Zero Disk Retention: Collections live in RAM (EphemeralClient).
  • ▹ Session Isolation: Keyed to unique session UUIDs.
  • ▹ LRU Eviction: Capped to 10 active collections to prevent bloat.
STAGE 04 src/chain.py

Two-Layer Guardrails

  • ▹ Layer 1 Gate: Cosine similarity threshold ($s \ge 0.48$).
  • ▹ Layer 2 Grounding: XML <document_context> isolation.
  • ▹ Conditional Rewrite: Rewrites history only on Turn 2+.
